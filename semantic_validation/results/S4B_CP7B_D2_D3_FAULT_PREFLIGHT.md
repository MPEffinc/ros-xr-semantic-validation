## Protocol-matched input and positive-motion feasibility

The protocol defines D2 as one second of `isTracked=false` after one second of active control with the **same pose and teleop**, and D3 as one second of **no source bytes** after one second active while the last state remains true. Source rate is 20 Hz. The [host fixture checker](runs/s4b_cp7b_fault_preflight_20260926T124900Z/analysis/fault_fixture.py) generated 120 planned 50 ms indices and passed exact static comparisons: indices 56–75 are D2's 20 tracked-false transmitted samples, or D3's 20 intentionally unsent slots. D2's pose, teleop, generation and planned time equal the matched tracked-true control in those slots; D3's last actual source is index 55 (`isTracked=true`, teleop=true), with next actual index 76. D2 would transmit 120 records, D3 100. Source monotonic generation timestamps, actual send times, in-flight messages and a moving-state trigger remain **UNKNOWN until runtime**. D3's 20 silent slots must not be relabelled as lost sender data or assigned phantom source sample IDs.

As a feasibility check only, [historical original D1 shim joints](runs/s4b_cp7b_fault_preflight_20260926T124900Z/analysis/historical_d1_motion.json) were reread within ±60 ms of the prospective 2.800 s fault onset. The five historical normal-input trials had maximum absolute joint velocities of roughly 0.183–0.191 rad/s in that window. This suggests the scheduled fault may occur during motion, but does **not** establish D2/D3 movement, equal initial state or positive controls. The actual fault runs must measure those independently; no threshold was changed to accommodate the observed D1 profile.
