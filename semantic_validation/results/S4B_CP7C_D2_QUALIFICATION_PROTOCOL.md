Ten first-attempt setup cells: B0, B0 observational shim, B1/B2-native/B2-composed/B3 each in I_NATIVE and I_FULL. Each has one initial integrated attempt and **at most two setup-only retries with the same frozen code/config/fixture**; stop after exhaustion. A frozen implementation change affecting control, input, metric or association invalidates affected cells and requires a separately registered campaign; no automatic Q6.
