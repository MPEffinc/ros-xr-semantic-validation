## Qualification start conditions and evidence boundary

This commit is the **new campaign runtime freeze point** once pushed and its remote SHA verified. CP4 runtime attempts per named cell are then limited to one initial plus two setup-only retries. The code, fixture, property, thresholds, analyses and retry handling above must not be changed after seeing a runtime outcome; an affected variant would require a new prospective version/freeze. Every attempt must have a unique result directory and retained stdout/stderr, source, monitor/oracle, ROS/Servo/controller, `/joint_states`, readiness/clock and exit logs. B0/observational B0-shim equivalence under the frozen 5 ms/0.02 rad rules must be rechecked for this changed CP4 configuration. A source→Servo callback exact-field match does **not** identify an individual controller output or Gazebo joint sample; those remain interval-level/UNKNOWN where no parent ID exists.

Preflight supports proceeding to **setup-only integrated qualification** after this freeze, not directly to formal comparison. B2 I_FULL CP3 setup02 motion is still invalid as a correct-policy positive control; CP3 setup03 remains sender setup failure. No B0–B3 defense performance comparison, ranking, S5 decision, Quest run or physical robot trial is reported here. Formal D1 requires every relevant arm's real positive control, original consumer and Servo callback evidence, full graph/clock barrier, source-index comparability, common stop contract where applicable, and a **second separate formal trial schedule/configuration commit** before five randomized repetitions. Other Docker/OpenVR cases remain blocked on their own prerequisites.
