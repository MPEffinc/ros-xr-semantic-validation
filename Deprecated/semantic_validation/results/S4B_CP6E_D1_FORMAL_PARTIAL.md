## Research interpretation and next gate

This campaign demonstrates that, under **synthetic Docker D1 normal inputs**, the original path and the implemented existing-defense paths can accept normal active input and move the simulated robot without observed steady-active false rejection in their valid subsets. It does **not** complete the registered five-valid-repeat four-baseline D1 comparison for the two B2 I_FULL arms, measure invalid tracking/freshness/stall/recovery defense efficacy, verify actual Quest semantics, or establish a method gap. B2-native versus B2-composed failure/stop behavior was **not** exercised by D1. Docker D2–D6/C-ID/C-MON still require separate event-level, stop and recovery qualifications; OpenVR retains observer-equivalence and jump-free re-arm blockers. **S4-B remains PARTIAL; S5 remains INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; S6/S7 were not started.**

No further formal trial is authorized under this frozen schedule without a prospective, bounded retry/observation-readiness extension. That extension must address property→downstream completeness without changing the original policy, preserve both invalid trials, freeze new code/IDs before runtime, and avoid choosing only favorable outcomes. Until then, there is no complete existing-defense comparison from which to rank methods or infer novelty.
